# Video preproduction

1. Confirm audience, objective, duration, aspect ratio, language, tone, distribution channel, and CTA.
2. Inspect the real product or approved evidence; list claims that can and cannot be shown.
3. Write a timed narrative with one purpose per scene.
4. Map each scene to footage, UI state, voiceover, captions, transition, and acceptance check.
5. Identify privacy, licensing, and capture constraints before production.
6. Obtain approval on script and storyboard before expensive generation or editing.
