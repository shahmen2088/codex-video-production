# Video QC and handoff

Read [the delivery checklist](../../references/delivery-checklist.md).

1. Compare final duration, scene order, claims, captions, and CTA with the approved storyboard.
2. Inspect technical metadata and watch the whole export with sound.
3. Check transitions, dead frames, frozen opening frames, audio clipping, subtitle timing, and final-frame completeness.
4. Package brief, script, storyboard, asset manifest, commands, source links, checksums, final exports, and known limitations.
5. Confirm the recipient can identify the final file and reproduce the build.
