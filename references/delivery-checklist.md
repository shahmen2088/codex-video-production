# Video delivery checklist

- duration and aspect ratio match the brief;
- no confidential UI, notifications, tokens, or personal data are visible;
- audio is intelligible and does not clip;
- captions match speech and remain inside safe areas;
- frame rate, resolution, codec, pixel format, and audio sample rate are documented;
- final output is inspected with `ffprobe` and played from start to finish;
- source manifest and reproducible commands are included.
